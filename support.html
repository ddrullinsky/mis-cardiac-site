About the app

MIS Cardiac is an educational reference for cardiac surgeons and surgical trainees on minimally invasive cardiac surgical approaches: transaxillary minithoracotomy, upper mini-sternotomy and MIDCAB. Each procedure has phase-by-phase checklists, decision points, safety checks and bailout protocols, summarised from cited peer-reviewed literature and society guidelines.
